Shorts Bot – Privacy Policy

Shorts Bot is a personal tool used only by the channel owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

What we access

Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope only).

What we store

A login token saved only on the owner's own computer. No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
